Late invoice interest in Bulgaria 2026

Interest = principal × (ECB MRO + 8 pp) × days / 360.

ECB MRO is 2.40% until 15 September 2026 and 2.65% from 16 September 2026.

Example: 1000 EUR, 68 days, 2.40% ECB. Rate 10.40%. Interest 19.64 EUR.

This is not legal advice and not for tax-authority interest.
