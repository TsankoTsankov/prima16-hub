Logo
- Files in repo: /favicon.svg and /brand/logo-wordmark.svg

- Blog header 1200x630
- Business card 85x55 mm print
- Email signature: wordmark + support@prima16.com + prima16.com

Layout rule for every post
1. One sentence problem at the top.
2. Number or price in the middle.
3. URL at the bottom in sans, no emoji, no em dash.
4. Square green button label drawn as a rectangle, not a pill.
